Who We Are

Hello, we are team BudjetTech #25049, a third-year high school robotics team from Dr. Norman Bethune Collegiate Institute, made up of 16 students from diverse backgrounds. We’ve built a close-knit, collaborative team driven by a shared passion for robotics. This passion fuels our creativity and strategic thinking in both designing our robot and engaging the community through outreach. Throughout the season, we challenge each other to think innovatively and solve problems together. Committed to giving back, we actively promote STEM in our community, inspiring others to explore robotics and technology and showing that everyone can make a difference.
